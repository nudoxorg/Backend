## The measured defect (manager-verified)

`MAX_REF_LIST_ELEMENTS = 16` bounds every pooled entity/atom/type list.
Real Go types exceed it: github.com/google/uuid's `encoder` carries 22
methods, and the projection folds the overflow into
`ProjectionFault::ListCapacity` -> `NoSupportedDeclaration` — a silent
module loss. The corpus is the measuring instrument; the bound must fit
measured demand. 32 covers every corpus row observed; if any row still
faults after the raise, STOP and report the largest observed set — do
not iterate the constant yourself.
