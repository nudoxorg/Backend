# Product rubric readiness

Status: **NOT ADOPTED — DO NOT SCORE THE PRODUCT**

The former Wave 1 scorecard was a useful historical opinion, but it was not calibrated under the
current reviewer and rubric-writer contracts. Keeping a numeric “final” score beside those stricter
contracts made it too easy for root or an agent to cite an invalid conclusion. Git history retains
that artifact; it is intentionally not an active rubric.

Full plan completion will remain eight and a same-direction, measured stretch will remain nine or
ten. No weights, aggregate, or product score are valid until every readiness row below is closed by
the process in `.codex/skills/write-evidence-rubric/SKILL.md`.

| Approved contract cards for every scored plane | Open | Root closure receipts for each prerequisite capability |

Until all rows close, use capability contracts, pass/fail laws, closure receipts, and blocker/major
findings. Do not assign a provisional number: missing proof is `UNVERIFIED`, not a low-confidence
fractional score.
