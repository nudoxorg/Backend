# FACET motion grammar (v4)

Live board: `Motion.html` (served at `http://127.0.0.1:47811/v4/motion/Motion.html`).
Every transition below plays there on real content. The pages are toml's `Value` and its real 0.8.23 → 1.1.6 history, `present::glyph` from this workspace, world names ranked for a real query, and csv 1.4.0 from the registry.

- **Stills.** `?demo=<id>&t=<ms>` renders the exact frame at `t`. Add `&reduced=1` for reduced motion and `&int=1` for the interrupted version.
- **Films.** They are in `../shots/motion/<id>.png`; regenerate them with `python3 films.py [ids] [--reduced] [--int]`.
- **Data.** `data.js` is generated by `python3 build.py`, which reads the world, `releases.json`, the registry index, `Cargo.lock` and the sources.

The board evaluates the curves, springs and retargets with the same maths as `facet::motion`: the WebKit bezier solver, the closed-form spring, value-kept tween retargets and velocity-kept spring retargets. A number read off the board is therefore the number to build.

## The rule

**No transition is a plain opacity fade.** A motion must say what happened between before and after: in, out, next, across, the same thing at another time, opened from this word, taken, broken. Any motion whose meaning cannot be said in one sentence is cut. Every demo on the board carries its sentence.

Five laws follow from the rule.

1. **What is the same holds still.** A token, name, stone or segment that is equal before and after never moves. This holds across siblings, across packages and across time. Only the difference moves.
2. **The motion ends where the thing now lives.** A peek comes out of the exact token, and a page comes out of the exact row. A copied install line drops into the clipboard slot under it. A pinned name lands in the hand. A package lands in the row it sorts into.
3. **One thing moves prominently.** Everything else is at most 8 px of travel, a clip or a colour.
4. **Everything is interruptible from where it is painted.** Springs keep their velocity. Tweens restart from their current value. Nothing snaps back and nothing restarts.
5. **Reduced motion marks instead of moving** (see [Reduced motion](#reduced-motion)).

Opacity is used for exactly one thing: a mark's colour settling under reduced motion. It never carries an entrance or an exit.

## Directions mean something

| Axis | Meaning | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| depth | in / out | the thing you chose *becomes* the destination; everything else spreads away from it (in) or closes around it (out); only the becoming element scales |
| list order | next / previous | reels roll up for the next item and down for the previous |
| time | later / earlier | forward in time rolls up and slides content in from the right; back in time rolls down and from the left |
| the world graph | another package | the page is pushed the way that package lies in the graph's layout; the ground slides along the true vector |
| reading order | left to right, top to bottom | wipes, staggers and sweeps follow reading order; the theme sweep follows the house light (top-left) |

## Tokens

Durations stay short. Most are 90–240 ms. Across takes 320 ms. The scrub head is the one long motion, because it is a timeline: `clamp(18 ms × releases + 120, 240, 640)`.

| Curve | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| glide | (.22,1,.36,1) | arriving, unrolling |
| snap | (.3,0,0,1) | crisp arrivals |
| drop | (.5,0,.9,.6) | leaving (accelerate) |
| spring | (.2,.9,.25,1.18) | the friend's pop only |
| bounce | (.34,1.56,.64,1) | not used for transitions (play uses springs and keys) |
| **timeline** (new) | (.4,0,.2,1) | the scrub head: steady through the middle, so every release is felt |

| Spring | response s, ζ | Use |
|---|---|---|
| SNAPPY | .28, .86 | FLIP rows, cards following an anchor, leading edges |
| GENTLE | .45, 1 | shelf width, text-size reflow |
| BOUNCY | .42, .6 | play only: add's room, the hand's landing |
| **CARRY** (new) | .34, 1 | one driver for a whole place change (deeper / back) |
| **REEL** (new) | .24, .92 | reels, odometer wheels, tick glyphs |
| **TRACK** (new) | .20, 1 | caret, matched-letters underline |
| **TRAIL** (new) | .50, 1 | wakes (the tail of a moved row) |
| **EDGE** (new) | .36, 1 | trailing edges (the inchworm underline and focus ring) |

## The vocabulary

Every transition is built from these verbs. Each is portable to GPUI's transform, opacity, clip, bounds and colour interpolation. Rotation happens only inside painted primitives.

| Verb | What moves | Transitions |
|---|---|---|
| **Become** | an element keyed the same at both ends morphs between their bounds (uniform scale for text) | deeper, back, take to hand, ask field → hero (D-Browse), door gem → focus gem (D-Page) |
| **Spread / close** | everything else moves away from (or back to) an origin in proportion to its distance | deeper, back, the door's gather (D-Page) |
| **Reel** | labels stacked in list order in a one-label window; a spring rolls them | sibling, jump-bar choice, find's preview, copy's tick, progress words |
| **Odometer** | wheels roll one step: versions per semver segment in time's direction; counts per digit in numeric direction, ones first | scrub, counts everywhere, sibling's line number |
| **Unfurl** | from an exact origin (a token's underline, a tick, a card's edge, a segment) an edge extends, then the body unrolls by clip | peek, chain, lens, jump menu |
| **Unroll / roll up** | a slot's width or height grows while a clip uncovers the content in place | x-ray, narrow, fold, scrub clauses, new rows |
| **Wipe** | two layers split by a boundary that travels against the movement, each offset 12 px | tabs, warm peek sweep, lens slide |
| **Push** | full-width travel, old and new side by side | across |
| **Travel** | an item goes to its new place (FLIP); a *wake* shows how far; an *inchworm* moves the leading edge first | find, tabs underline, focus ring, selection bars |
| **Throw / drop** (play) | lift, arc, room, squash and settle | add, copy's drop, the hand's landing |
| **Turn** | a stone turns one symmetry step under the fixed top-left light, its facets taking the light of where they pass, and ends looking as it started | across, copy's tick, add's flight |
| **Light** | facets light per unit of real work; the newest flashes; one glint on seal; light travels the edge while working | progress, theme landing |
| **Crack / pulse** | a failed facet turns coral and a crack is drawn; the exact operand pulses once | fault |
| **Sweep** | palette roles mix region by region, delayed from the top-left | theme |
| **Anchor** | an epoch keeps the element you were reading fixed while the rest flows | text size |

## The transitions

Each record lists the relation it encodes, the choreography in order, the timing, what happens on interruption, the reduced-motion version, and the GPUI mapping. The existing primitive is named first. **New:** marks a primitive `motion::` or gpui lacks today.

### scrub: the same thing at another time (the centrepiece)

Film: `scrub.png`, `scrub-int.png`, `scrub-reduced.png`

The motion carries what no still can: clicking 1.1.6 does not jump. The head *walks the 28 releases* between your pin (0.8.23) and 1.1.6. Every change lands as the head crosses the release that made it, so watching the scrub is reading the history.

- **Driver.** Head position `s`, in releases.
  - Click: a tween to the target over `clamp(18 ms × n + 120, 240, 640)` on the timeline curve. For toml, n = 28, so the tween takes 624 ms.
  - Drag: `s` is the pointer.
  - Esc: back to the pin at 12 ms per release.
- **Comb.** A tick flashes from ink3 towards periwinkle-high as the head crosses it and stays lit (the path you scrubbed).
- **Rider.** The number rides the head (D-Marks' rider). Only the semver wheel that changed rolls, one step per release crossed, so 0.9.0 → 0.9.12 spins the patch wheel and 0.9.12 → 1.0.0 rolls all three.
- **Beats, from real data.** The registry index gives every release; `releases.json` gives the local APIs.
  - **0.9.0:** `toml_edit` is struck (0–45 % of 200 ms), then closes, while `toml_parser`, `toml_writer` and `winnow` unroll in its place.
  - **0.9.6:** `serde` grows its new syllable `_core`, underlined mint.
  - **0.9.7:** Rust 1.66 rolls to 1.76.
  - **1.1.0:** Rust 1.76 rolls to 1.85.
  - **1.1.5** (the first local release after the pin):
    - The section "Upgrading to 1.1.5" grows (220 ms, pushing "one of" down).
    - The row "added deserialize_struct" grows, and its mint underline draws.
    - The shelf's counts roll from 0 to 43 / 77 / 35.
  - **1.1.6:** only the section title's patch wheel rolls, because the API did not change.
- **Holds.** The hero, facts, variants and shelf rows never move. They are only pushed by a section's height.
- **The lens.** It opens as soon as `s` leaves the pin: "you pin 0.8.23 · esc", 160 ms height.
- **Interruption.** Reversing mid-scrub (`int`) retargets the head from where it is. Beats are keyed to crossings, not to time, so each one un-happens as the head recrosses its tick.
- **Reduced.** The head and every value cut to the target. The built-on line and the section title hold a mint mark for 1.2 s.
- **GPUI.**
  - `Motion::animate("scrub.s", Spec::tween)` drives the head.
  - Each beat is a `Presence` entry (Axis::Horizontal for clauses, Vertical for rows) whose enter and exit are fired by crossings.
  - **New:** *Roll* and *Odometer*; *beat track* (thresholds on a driver emit presence events in both directions); *clip act*; *line draw* (the strike, the underline).

### deeper: the row becomes the page

Film: `deeper.png`, `deeper-int.png`, `deeper-reduced.png`

The clicked row is the origin, so you always know where you came from.

- **Driver.** One track `p` from 0 to 1 on CARRY (about 360 ms, no overshoot). Every pose is a band of `p`, and bands stay linear because the driver is already a spring.
- **Become** (`p` 0–.6):
  - The row's mark grows into the hero stone. Its facets light from .1 to .55: at mark size it is only its glyph.
  - The name grows into the title.
  - The row's sentence becomes the lede (`p` .1–.7, trailing the name so they never cross). It is the same text: the doc's first line.
- **Spread** (`p` 0–.75). Every other element moves by `(y − y_row) × 9p`: a zoom whose origin is the row. The nearest neighbours leave through the reader's edge first. Nothing scales and nothing fades.
- **Unfold** (`p` .55–1, once the hero has landed). The body is revealed downward from the hero's baseline (clip + 10 px drop).
- **Shelf.** glyph's children unroll (`p` .05–.6). The mint bar travels into RelationLabel (`p` .15–.85).
- **Jump bar and address.** Each grows one segment (`p` .45–1).
- **Interruption.** Back at 170 ms retargets `p` to 0 from its painted value and velocity.
- **Reduced.** Cut. The new jump-bar segment holds a periwinkle mark for 1.2 s.
- **GPUI.**
  - `shared` (Fit::Height) for the three morphs, `Offset` for the spread, `Reveal` for the unfold.
  - **New:** *Shared driven by an external progress*, so deeper, back and interruption share one retargetable track (today a Shared morph owns its own tween).
- **Replaces** in `desktop/src/shell/reader.rs`:
  - `enter_act` (Way::Down, GraphPage): 24 px rise, 0.985 scale and a fade.
  - `exit_act`: 0.99 scale and a fade.

### back: out the way you came

Film: `back.png`

- **Rule.** The history entry remembers the relation that brought you there, and Back plays that relation's motion with its driver reversed. `Place.way` in `reader.rs` already records Down, Up, Across, View and GraphPage; it needs to drive the reverse instead of Way::Up's own entrance.
- **Choreography.** The same `p` goes 1 → 0 on CARRY:
  1. The body folds first (`p` 1–.55).
  2. The hero flies home into its row (`p` .6–0).
  3. The list closes around it (`p` .7–0).
- **Mark.** The row you came from keeps a periwinkle tint (alpha 0.09) from 180 to 700 ms, then lets go over 240 ms. It is colour only: *this is where you were*.
- **Reduced.** Cut. The row holds the mark.
- **GPUI.** As deeper. **New:** *arrival relation drives Back*.

### sibling: the reel

Film: `sibling.png`

- **Reel.** The name, the lede, the stone's glyph and the jump bar's last segment are reels in list order. `pos` springs on REEL. A second ⌥↓ at 140 ms retargets it with velocity kept, so fast stepping *spins past* the item you skipped (relation_label on the way to typed_label).
- **Holds.** The stone keeps its place and size. Only its hue tweens (type-green to call-blue) and its glyph rolls inside the table. The module, the tabs and the section order hold too.
- **Facts.** Differing tokens roll. The kind word rolls, and the line number and use count roll as counts.
- **Body.** It folds up into the hero during the first 40 % of a step and the next body unfolds during the last 60 %. Two bodies are never shown at once.
- **Shelf.** The mint bar travels on the same spring.
- **Reduced.** Cut. The title holds a periwinkle mark.
- **GPUI.** **New:** *Roll* (a content mask plus `Offset` on a keyed column); the rest is `Reveal` and colour.

### across: new ground

Film: `across.png`

The move is toml::Value → serde_json::Value.

- **Direction.** The unit vector from toml to serde_json in the world graph's layout, `(0.99, −0.16)`, which is east and a little north.

- **Turn.** The package stone and the hero stone turn one quarter over the push. The geometry rotates while each facet takes the light of the place it passes, and the stone ends lit exactly as it started.
- **Reduced.** Cut. The shelf book holds a periwinkle edge mark.
- **GPUI.**
  - `Offset` and content masks.

### peek: the word, opened

Film: `peek.png`, `peek-reduced.png`

- **Grow.**
  1. The underline draws under the exact token (0–90 ms).
  2. The underline *becomes the card's top edge*: its x, width and y travel to the card's (60–200 ms, glide). It stays as the card's periwinkle hairline.
  3. The body unrolls from that edge (clip, 140–320 ms).

  Content is never scaled or faded.
- **Warm sweep.** Resting on RelationDirection:
  - The card's x springs to the new anchor on SNAPPY, and its height springs to the new content.
  - The content wipes in reading order (160 ms): the boundary travels right to left, old content leaves by 12 px, and new content arrives from 12 px.
  - The old underline undraws while the new one draws.
- **Leave.**
  1. The body rolls up into its edge (120 ms, drop).
  2. The edge shrinks back to the word (90 ms).
  3. The underline undraws (90 ms).
- **Interruption.** Resting again mid-exit reverses from where it is (the float layer already reverses exits).
- **Reduced.** The card cuts in and out, and the token keeps its underline while the card is open.
- **GPUI.** The float layer's placement, hover intent and chain stay. **New:** *unfurl* entrance (card bounds morph from the anchor's underline rect plus `Reveal` from the anchored edge) and *wipe* swap.
- **Replaces** in `overlay/float.rs`:

  - The warm swap's `draw.swap` opacity cross-fade.
  - `presence::act::PEEK` and `keys::PEEK`.

### find: the list narrows around what you type

Film: `find.png`

The query types `fro` through to `from_str`; the ranking comes from real world names.

- **Travel.** It runs as `Flow`, simulated on a 2 ms grid exactly as `facet::motion::flow` does:
  - The layout is the flow of current row heights in rank order.
  - A leaving row keeps its place while it closes.
  - A key is an epoch whose jump is absorbed into each row's SNAPPY spring.
  - There is no stagger, because typing must never lag.
- **Wake.** A 1.5 px periwinkle line runs from where the row was to where it is. Its tail follows on TRAIL, so *the wake's length is the rank change*: from_str's rise from third to first shows as a long line. The wake shrinks to nothing as the row settles, and the motion says what the new letter did.
- **Narrowing first.** Leavers close in 100 ms (drop). Arrivals open 60 ms later at their rank (160 ms glide, rising 4 px), so they never share a slot.
- **Letters.** The typed character drops 4 px into its slot (90 ms). The caret and the matched-letters underline follow on TRACK.
- **Count and preview.**
  - The count is an odometer: one step, ones first.
  - The preview is a reel of top results, so it moves only when the top result changes: `From` at "from", `from_slice` at "from_", `from_str` at "from_st".
- **Reduced.** Rows cut to their ranks. No wake.
- **GPUI.** `Flow` and `Presence`. **New:** *wake* (a trailing-spring line per keyed Flow item).

### textsize: the line you read stays put

Film: `textsize.png`

- **Anchor.** The element at the reading line (here the "Made of" heading) keeps its top-left. The scroll offset is chosen so this holds at the new size.
- **Flow.** Every keyed element springs from its old bounds to its new ones on GENTLE. Text scales through the compositing layer, so glyphs are re-rasterized, never stretched. Wraps that change land at the end.
- **Reduced.** The new size appears at once, still anchored. The anchor is where you are, not motion.
- **GPUI.** `Flow` with `Resize::Scale` on a text-scale epoch exists. **New:** *scroll anchor across an epoch*.

- **Content.** A wipe in the same direction (200 ms glide, 12 px). The Relations tab speaks DIRECTIONS §4's sentence.

## Reduced motion

A pure cut throws away the relation, and a cross-dissolve is the fade we are removing.

Under reduced motion nothing translates, scales or clips progressively. States change at once, and **what changed carries a mark**: a held colour for 1.2 s, then a 160 ms settle.

| Mark | Meaning | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| periwinkle | where you arrived | the new jump-bar segment |
| periwinkle | where you were | the row you came back to |
| mint | what is new | added rows, the scrubbed section, the copied line |
| coral | what broke | the fault's operand |

Information-bearing state still updates, for example facets lighting for progress. The films `*-reduced.png` show deeper, scrub, add and peek.

## Primitives `motion::` / gpui lacks today

1. **Roll.** A keyed column in a content mask, offset by a spring. It gives reels and is the basis of Odometer: per-segment or per-digit wheels, width interpolation, and direction rules.
2. **Clip act.** `Pose` gains reveal insets, so `Presence` acts uncover in place instead of fading (x-ray, narrow, fold, rows, scrub clauses).
3. **Unfurl** (float layer). Card bounds morph from the anchor rect (an underline, a tick, a parent's edge, a segment), with `Reveal` from the anchored edge.
4. **Wipe.** Two content masks split by a travelling boundary, plus 12 px offsets.
5. **Shared driven by an external progress.** One CARRY track for deeper and back, with retargeting.
6. **Arrival relation on history entries** that drives Back. This is partly present as `Place.way`.
7. **Beat track.** Thresholds on a driver emit enter and leave events in both directions (the scrub).
8. **Gem** additions:
   - `turn(facets)`: geometry rotation with per-position light;
   - `flash` on the newest facet;
   - a one-shot `glint`;
   - `crack(progress)`.
9. **Arc flight.** A two-axis path over one progress (throw, take), with landing keys.
10. **Line draw.** A painted rule with draw progress: underline, strike, connector, join, crack.
11. **Wake.** A trailing-spring line per keyed Flow item.
12. **Edge-split bounds.** Leading and trailing edges on two springs (focus ring, tab underline).
13. **Scroll anchor across an epoch** (text size, density, room).
14. **Per-region palette mix with a delay** (the theme sweep).
15. **Ground offset** (across parallax).
16. **Reduced-motion mark.** A keyed colour hold, 1.2 s plus a 160 ms settle.

## Build order (biggest felt win first)

1. **Peek unfurl and wipe** (float layer). It is the most frequent transition, so it removes the fade people see most.
2. **Roll and Odometer.** They are cheap and serve sibling, counts, versions, preview, ticks and words.
3. **Deeper and back** on one CARRY driver: become, spread and unfold, with Back from `Place.way`. This replaces reader.rs's fades.
4. **Clip act** for Presence: x-ray, fold, narrow and rows all fall out of it.
5. **Scrub timeline.** Beat track plus Roll, Odometer and clip act. It is the showpiece: motion that carries history.
6. **Find:** Flow springs, narrowing first, and the wake.
7. **Across:** push, Gem::turn and ground offset.
8. **Take:** arc flight for add, copy's slot and the hand's join (line draw).
9. **Progress and fault:** flash, glint, crack and pulse-once.
10. **Theme sweep, text-size anchor, and the tabs and focus inchworm.**
