The bevel is the only state channel

No status dots, no badges on symbols. A plate has one lit edge, and that edge is where state lives. It thickens, runs, or changes hue; the content never moves.

Plain until touched

Dense things rest as texture and answer the pointer with a wave: the tick you touch rises, its neighbours follow, and a small plate names it. The same gesture reads versions, languages, files and trails.
