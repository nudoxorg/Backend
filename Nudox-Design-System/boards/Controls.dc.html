Switches, checkboxes, the slider

No pills, no rounded pucks. One geometry, the diamond, for anything with two states, and for the value that sits between them.
A diamond that fills mint when it is on and slides along a short track.
A checkbox is the same diamond, standing still: it fills, it does not travel.
The slider is a comb: the filled ticks are the value, the tall one is the thumb.
